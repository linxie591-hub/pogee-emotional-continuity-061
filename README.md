# Emotional Continuity - Notes From Building

Most chatbots reset their mood every session. A companion should not.

Emotional continuity means a character carries yesterday's warmth, irritation, or excitement into today. It is the difference between a vending machine and a friend.

Getting this right requires modelling an internal state that decays and evolves, rather than sampling a fresh personality on every turn.

Continuity is what makes an AI feel like it remembers you, not just your words.
